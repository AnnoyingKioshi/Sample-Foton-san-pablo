One lot on the National Highway.

Serving San Pablo City and the towns around the Seven Lakes, from a single property where sales, financing and service sit together.

Foton Philippines San Pablo sells and services the Foton commercial lineup — Thunder, Tornado, Traveller, Gratour and Transvan — from one lot along the National Highway in San Pablo City, Laguna.

Most buyers here run a business. Rice traders moving sacks toward Sta. Cruz, delivery operators serving Calamba and Los Baños, cooperatives shuttling workers out to the coconut and coffee farms past the city proper. The lineup is stocked around that kind of driving: short-haul, loaded, daily.

Sales, financing and the service bays share the same property, so a unit bought here gets serviced here, by technicians who know the exact trims sold on this lot.
